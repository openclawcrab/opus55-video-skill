From brief to finished film.

You talk to Claude Code as usual, with Claude Opus 5.5 selected as the model. The skill gives it the renderer, the sound engine, the style references and the checks, so the result is a file you can post, not a storyboard.

  1. 1

    Describe the brief

    Brand, product, any copy or photos, and where it will run. Write it in any language.

  2. 2

    Pick a style

    Choose one of the 22, or let Claude score them against your brief and pick.

  3. 3

    Plan from your content

    Each beat gets enough time to be read. The hero moment lands on the music's downbeat.

  4. 4

    Render picture and sound

    Frames are drawn in headless Chrome; the soundtrack and effects are composed to match the cuts.

  5. 5

    Check before delivery

    Duration, size, loudness and true peak are measured; clipped text, overlaps and dead frames are flagged.

Questions.

Which Claude model should I use?

Claude Opus 5.5. We built and tested the skill with it, and every example film on this page was made with it: planning the beats, writing each frame, fixing layout problems and judging the review. Other models can run the skill, but we have not tested them to the same standard.

What do I need to run it?

Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5 selected, Node.js 18 or newer, Python 3.10 or newer with numpy and scipy, ffmpeg, and Google Chrome or Chromium. A setup check tells you exactly what is missing and how to fix it.

Do I need an image generator?

No. Many styles are drawn entirely in code. Photo-led styles look best with your own product shots or images from a generator you have access to; without either, they fall back to drawn imagery.

Can I use it for my own brand?

Yes. Give it your brand name, copy and photos you hold the rights to. Every brand in the example films is invented; the skill refuses to imitate real fashion houses or their logos.

How long does a film take?

A 10 second 1080p film renders in about a minute on a recent laptop. Planning, review and revisions with Claude take longer, depending on how much you want to change.

Who owns the films I make?

The films you make with the skill are yours to use. If you use AI-generated images inside them, the terms of that image provider also apply.

Can I edit a film after it renders?

Yes. Each film is a folder with a content plan, a page and an audio script. Ask Claude to change copy, timing, colours or a single scene and render again.
