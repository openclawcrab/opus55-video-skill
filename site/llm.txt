# Fashion Video Skill for Claude Code

> A Claude Code skill that turns a fashion brief into a finished 6 to 15 second brand film: picture, motion, music and sound effects, all made in code. 22 styles, landscape and vertical. Built and tested with Claude Opus 5.5.

Fashion Video Skill (v2.0.2) is installed into Claude Code. Give Claude a brand, a product and a platform; the skill plans the film from that content, draws every frame in headless Chrome, composes music and sound effects, and checks the result (duration, size, loudness at -14 LUFS, true peak, clipped or overlapping text) before handing over an MP4. Output sizes: 16:9, 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1. Recommended model: Claude Opus 5.5; every example film on the site was made with it.
